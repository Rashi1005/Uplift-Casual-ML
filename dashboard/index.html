What the result means

The ranking is useful for experimentation and business planning, but it should not be treated as proof that the top model will always win in a future campaign.

A future decision should ideally include a new randomized holdout group and measure incremental outcomes again.

The persuadables problem

A standard response model predicts who is likely to respond. Uplift modeling asks a different question: who responds because they received the intervention?

Why the naive model is not enough

A response model can rank customers by their overall probability of visiting, but it cannot directly observe both outcomes for the same person: what happened with the email and what would have happened without it.
